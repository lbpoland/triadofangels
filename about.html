Our Story

Triad of Angels began as a music project built around three digital female figures — Samantha, Jessica, and Sonya — and it still makes the most sense from the music outward.

Brand originUmbrella structure

Triad of Angels grew from a real-world triplet origin into a more stylized, mythic, digital form. The full name matters: Triad of Angels. Music was the first expression of the project and it remains the clearest one.

ToA Studios grew as the wider home around that work. It began as the lane for music outside the main Triad of Angels identity and now carries publishing, video, future interactive work, digital products, and other studio-led releases.

They are not two disconnected brands. Triad of Angels is the front-facing creative identity. ToA Studios is the studio structure behind it and beyond it.

Albums, lyrics, and listening stay public and easy to reach.

Our Creative Process & Workflow

Modern digital tools can assist the workflow, but authorship, editing, selection, and release judgment stay human-led.

Human-ledAI-assisted

The work is not built on autopilot. Songs are shaped deliberately. Lyrics, arrangement, tone, artwork decisions, and release standards are all handled with intent.

Some releases lean worshipful. Some lean cinematic. Some push further into experimentation. What ties them together is not the software. It is the sense of direction behind the choices.

The aim is simple: make songs, stories, and creative work that feel sincere, strong, and worth returning to.

Music creation

Suno (AI music generation), DAWs, Adobe Audition, and eMastered for mastering—built around pro arrangement: full song structures (intros, verses, choruses, bridges, instrumentals, breakdowns), genre-authentic sound design, and lyrics written in-house for every song and album.

Visuals & video

RunwayML for generative video and editing, Hedra AI for lip-syncing, Revid AI for smart editing, Blender, Unreal Engine, and Unity for 3D content and game development, DaVinci Resolve for pro video work and music videos, and Adobe tools for art and content.

Ownership

While using cutting-edge AI, all lyrics, song layouts, melodies, and arrangements are original—and copyright belongs to Triad of Angels & ToA Studios.

Genres we cover

Gospel, Worship, Christian, Cinematic, Pop, Rock, Metal, Heavy Metal, Ballads, Country, Jazz, Funk, Hip Hop, Rap, R&B, Dance, Trance, EDM, Drum & Bass, Dubstep, Uplifting Trance, Hard Trance, Psytrance, Techno, Alternative, Classical, and more.

Future

We’re expanding into interactive games (web, Android, iOS), digital art, ebooks, and all digital products.
