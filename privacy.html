1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information you voluntarily provide to us, such as your name, email address, and any messages you send when contacting us or subscribing to updates. Like most sites, basic server logs may capture limited technical details (for example, IP address, browser type, and device information) for security and operational reliability. We do not run custom on-site analytics trackers by default.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and messages
  • Provide access to music, apps, and digital products
  • Improve our website and services
  • Send newsletters or promotional material (only if you opted in)

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share information when it is necessary to respond to you (for example, via email delivery) or when you choose to interact with third-party services embedded or linked from this site (for example, YouTube). Those third parties operate under their own policies.

4. Cookies & Tracking

This site does not run third-party analytics trackers by default. However, some third-party embeds and platforms you choose to use (for example, YouTube) may set cookies or collect usage data under their own policies.

Games & local storage: our browser games store game saves, settings, and input bindings locally on your device using IndexedDB (a standard browser storage feature). This data never leaves your device unless you export it as a file. You can clear it at any time by clearing site data for triadofangels.com in your browser settings.

5. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but take all reasonable steps to safeguard your data.

6. Links to Other Sites

Our website may link to third-party sites (such as Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and external stores). We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. We recommend reviewing their policies separately.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data. To make a request, please email contact@triadofangels.com.

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us, and we will take steps to delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted here with the effective date.

10. Contact Us

Contact Information

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: contact@triadofangels.com.
